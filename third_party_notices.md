## R packages

Aurora relies on open-source R packages developed and maintained by the R community. These packages are installed separately and remain subject to their respective licences and copyright notices.

A review of the declared licences of Aurora's direct and indirect R package dependencies was conducted on 24 November 2026 using R version 4.4.3. The review encompassed 131 packages, including 21 direct and 110 indirect dependencies.

No apparent licence incompatibilities with Aurora's GPL-3.0 licence were identified for the current distribution model.

A record of the reviewed packages, their versions, declared licences and links to their respective CRAN pages is provided in [dependency_licences.csv](dependency_licences.csv).
